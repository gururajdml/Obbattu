OBBATTU.IN WEBSITE
==================
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload all files/folders inside the extracted "obbattu-website" folder to your hosting public_html folder.
3. Make sure index.html is directly inside public_html.
4. Visit your domain.
5. Enable SSL/HTTPS in your hosting control panel.

IMPORTANT:
This package is a ready-to-upload front-end/demo store. The cart works with browser localStorage.
For REAL orders, customer accounts, database storage, Razorpay/UPI payments, admin panel and email/WhatsApp automation, a backend/payment integration must be added before launch.

Replace "Add your number" and other placeholder business details with your actual details.
